# Free EU Accessibility Statement Template

A plain-language template for a website accessibility statement under the
European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882) and the Web Accessibility
Directive (Directive (EU) 2016/2102).

The template is deliberately short. A statement that nobody maintains is worse
than a short one that is dated, owned and accurate.

> This is a template, not legal advice. It does not certify WCAG conformance.
> Check the national transposition in the country where you operate.

## What is inside

- [`template.md`](template.md) — fill-in-the-blank statement with the seven
  sections authorities and users expect.
- [`checklist.md`](checklist.md) — a six-point self-check for a statement you
  have already published.
- [`example.md`](example.md) — a fictional German webshop example.
- [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) — CC0 1.0; copy and adapt without attribution.

## Quick start

1. Copy `template.md` into your own repository or CMS.
2. Replace every `[bracketed]` field with a real fact.
3. Publish it at a stable URL, for example `/accessibility`.
4. Link to it from every page footer and from your checkout.
5. Put the next review date in a calendar and assign an owner.

## Free generator and checker

If you want the same structure filled from a short form, there is a free
browser-only generator:

- Generator: <https://a11ystatement.pages.dev/>
- Six-point self-check: <https://a11ystatement.pages.dev/checker>

The generator does not upload what you type. Monitoring is an optional paid
service; the template and checker are free.

## Scope and caveats

- The European Accessibility Act applies to many consumer-facing services in
  the EU from 28 June 2025. It does not prescribe one sentence for every site.
- Public-sector websites in the EU follow the Web Accessibility Directive
  (EU) 2016/2102 and its national implementations.
- The EAA does not apply in Great Britain. UK public-sector bodies follow
  separate UK accessibility regulations.
- WCAG 2.2 level AA is the technical benchmark most authorities reference,
  usually through EN 301 549.
- A statement is a description of the current state, not a certificate. Test
  the actual user journey before claiming conformance.
