# Example: fictional German webshop

This example is illustrative. Muster GmbH does not exist, and the limitations
are not a real client's list.

```text
Accessibility Statement
Muster GmbH — https://shop.muster.example
Last updated: 24 October 2026
Next review due: 24 October 2027

1. Commitment
Muster GmbH is working to make https://shop.muster.example usable by everyone,
including people who rely on assistive technology. We aim to conform to
WCAG 2.2 level AA.

2. Scope
This statement covers https://shop.muster.example and its subpages. It does not
cover third-party content embedded from other providers.

3. Current status
We reviewed the site against WCAG 2.2 level AA on 24 October 2026. Where we fall
short, it is listed in section 5.

4. Feedback and contact
If you meet a barrier, write to accessibility@muster.example. We aim to reply
within five working days and to provide the information in an accessible format
on request.

5. Known limitations
1. PDF documents published before 2024 are not yet tagged.
2. The checkout date picker is keyboard accessible, but the error summary is
   not yet announced by every screen reader.

6. Enforcement
If our reply does not resolve the issue, you can contact the market surveillance
authority responsible for accessibility in Germany.

7. About this statement
Prepared on 24 October 2026. Reviewed: 24 October 2026. Next review due:
24 October 2027.
```

## Why this example passes the six-point check

- The contact is a named mailbox, not a generic sales form.
- The review date is in the future and has an owner.
- The scope names the domain and excludes third-party embeds.
- The conformance target is a specific standard and version.
- The limitations are specific and dated.
- The enforcement route names the responsible authority.

## What it still does not prove

The example does not prove that the shop works with a screen reader, keyboard,
200% zoom or high contrast. A statement describes the current state; only a
real accessibility test can support a conformance claim.
